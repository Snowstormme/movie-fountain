Privacy

Updated November 8, 2026. Movie Fountain has no accounts, advertising or application analytics.

Your movie lists & preferences

Watchlist movie metadata, watched movie IDs, custom profile avatar, and your theme preference (Classic or Liquid Glass) are stored locally in your browser’s localStorage. They are never uploaded or synchronized. You can remove saved movies, toggle watched marks, reset your avatar, or clear this site’s browser data to erase your lists and preferences.

Import, export and sharing

Watchlist JSON files are read locally and merged into your list. Export saves a copy to your device. Anyone you give that file to can read your selections. Sharing a movie sends only its public link through your chosen application, or copies it to your clipboard.

Requests and providers

Search terms, genre, year and duration filters, and movie or person identifiers are sent to our Cloudflare Pages Functions API, TMDB, and external rating providers (What's on? API and optional OMDb) to retrieve results. Posters and photos load from TMDB. Cloudflare and upstream providers may process IP addresses and request metadata under their own policies. This application does not intentionally store search history or server-side movie lists.

Third-party data and trademarks

Movie Fountain uses the TMDB API for movie metadata and poster artwork, but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. YouTube video trailers are embedded via YouTube's official privacy-enhanced embed player (youtube-nocookie.com). Movie Fountain is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Letterboxd Limited, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, or Google/YouTube. All trademarks and ratings are the property of their respective owners.

Choices and contact

Browser settings control local storage and reduced motion. Clearing site data permanently removes local selections unless you exported a backup. For questions, use the project repository; please do not post private information or API keys in public issues.
